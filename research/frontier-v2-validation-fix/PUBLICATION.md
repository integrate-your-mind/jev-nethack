# Frontier v2 bounded trial outcome

The isolated, preregistered successor trial completed all four 32-action arms. Frontier-v2 reached 14 more prefix-new positions than baseline across the two frozen branch points, but the preregistered classification is **negative** because the priority safety gate failed at step 3382 and the menu-label safety gate failed at step 4017. The latter action did successfully answer the direction prompt and open the door; this counterevidence does not change the frozen gate result.

The run used 143 of the 192 aggregate attempt ceiling, with 143 validated responses and no failed calls. Exact replay used zero provider calls. All 128 raw transitions, 128 full 121-action probability distributions, 15 goal distributions, requests, responses, and isolated NLE ttyrec footage remain preserved.

This is exploratory development evidence. It does not authorize a live policy change, retuning, extension, or rerun.
